# Clock Cycle

- The CPU has *clock pins* connected to a *clock generator*

- The clock generator oscillates between low and high voltage measured as 0 and
1

- A *clock cycle* is the time passed b/w two consecutive highs or lows

- The *clock speed* is the number of clock cycles finished per second

- The clock speed is determined by a *quartz-crystal circuit*

## Example

Suppose a 1 GHz CPU that finishes one instruction per cycle

- The *clock speed* of the CPU is $1\ GHz$

- The *clock cycle* of the CPU is $1\ ns$ ($10^{-9}\ sec$)

- The CPU executes $10^9$ (1 billion) instructions per second
